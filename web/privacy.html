Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 June 2026

Charity Billionaire, operated by Golden Home Project, LLC ("we" or "us"), is built to collect as little about you as possible. The website is a static page. There is no account, no sign-up, and no form. We do not ask for, store, or sell your name, email, or any other personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal information. You do not create an account or provide identifying details to use this site. We do not run advertising or third-party tracking or analytics scripts, and we do not set tracking cookies.

2. Blockchain data is public

Charity Billionaire operates on a public blockchain. When you deposit, withdraw, or are selected in a draw, that activity — including your wallet address and amounts — is recorded permanently on-chain and is publicly visible by design. This is inherent to public blockchains and is not controlled by us. Your wallet address is a pseudonym, not your legal identity, unless you reveal it elsewhere.

3. Third-party infrastructure

To display live figures and to let you interact with the contract, your browser connects to third-party services such as a blockchain RPC provider, your own wallet software, and the host that serves this page. These providers may, as a technical necessity, observe network information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or retain that information.

4. No selling of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, because we do not collect any.

5. Children

This service is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy may be directed to goldenhomeprojectllc@gmail.com.
