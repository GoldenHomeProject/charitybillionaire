The Mechanism

Three movements, one principle.

I

You Deposit

Add any amount to the shared pool. It is recorded as yours and withdrawable at any moment — no lock-ups, no permission required.

II

The Pool Earns

Pooled deposits earn interest. Only that interest is ever drawn — your principal is never touched, never at stake.

III

One Wins, All Give

Each week a single depositor receives 90% of the interest. 5% goes to a vetted charity; 5% sustains the platform. Every distribution is on-chain.

An Assurance

Your principal is yours, by design.

This is not a wager. There is no ticket to buy and nothing to lose. Your deposit is held by an open, non-custodial contract that no operator can reach — withdraw the full amount whenever you wish. The prize is funded purely by the pool's interest, which is why a deposit can win without ever being spent.

  • Withdraw anytimeFull principal, on demand.
  • Equal odds per dollarEvery dollar holds one equal, verifiable chance.
  • Provably fairWinners drawn by Chainlink VRF — cryptographically verifiable.
  • On-chain transparencyEvery prize and gift is publicly recorded.
